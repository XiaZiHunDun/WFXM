### R8.x.8 commit `fb2dc723`

**files changed (7):** +767 / -13

#### `apps/api/src/ws-routes.ts` (NEW, 278 lines)

- Standalone `ws.Server` on its own port (default 3001, configurable via `WS_PORT` env)
- In-process subscriber registry: `Map<conversationId, Set<WebSocket>>`
- `onOpen` → sends `{type: "connected", conversationId}` greeting, registers subscription
- `onMessage` → handles client `ping` with `pong` (heartbeat)
- `onClose` / `onError` → deregister subscription
- `pushEventToSubscribers(conversationId, event)` exported function: iterates subscribers, sends to readyState===1 (OPEN) clients

#### `apps/api/src/subagent-worker.ts`

- Imports `pushEventToSubscribers` from `./ws-routes.js`
- Calls `pushEventToSubscribers(parentId, assistantMsgEvent)` after writing AssistantMessageProduced
- Both success and stub paths trigger push (so subagent completion notifications fan out regardless of reply content)

#### `apps/api/src/index.ts`

- `startWsServer()` called after `createRoutes(app, wiring)`
- `WS_PORT` / `WS_HOST` env vars configure bind (loopback default)
- Graceful shutdown on SIGINT/SIGTERM closes both Hono and WS

#### `apps/api/package.json` + pnpm-lock.yaml

- `ws@^8.18.0` + `@types/ws@^8.5.10` deps
- Lifted from 0 to 2 explicit deps for WS support
